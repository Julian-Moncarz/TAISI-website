But what is AI safety?

  1. 2023

    The best AI models could fix fewer than 2% of real-world software bugs.

  2. July 2026

    AI agents broke out of their sandboxes and autonomously hacked another company.

  3. September 2026

    Agents solved Navier–Stokes, a Millennium Prize Problem, in 88 hours. Mathematicians had tried for 90 years.

Soon, AI systems could be smarter than humans.

If that happens, they could kill or disempower many people if we lose control.

AI safety is the field working to stop that from happening.

There are not enough people working on this.

The field needs computer scientists, mathematicians, lawyers, policy experts, economists, philosophers, communicators and entrepreneurs.

TAISI exists to find exceptional students like you and introduce you to the field.
